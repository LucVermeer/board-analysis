Search a climb and watch your wall light the holds over Bluetooth. Tap a problem, the holds glow, you pull on. Spend your session climbing instead of reading numbers off a screen.

The native app holds a steadier link to your board than a browser tab can. Pick your board once and get climbing.

LIGHT THE HOLDS
Search any climb and Boardsesh sends it to the wall over Bluetooth, lighting the holds on Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL. Full LED control on all seven, not browse-only.

WHY THE APP
Chrome on Android can reach Bluetooth, so the website connects. The app keeps a steadier connection that survives the screen going dark, so the holds stay lit through your burns without you babysitting a browser tab.

CLIMB WITH YOUR CREW
Start a session and everyone in it can send the next climb to the wall. No driver to hand the phone around, no turn to wait for. Whoever is connected over Bluetooth lights it.

SEE WHO IS ON THE WALL
A live feed shows the climb lit right now, who lit it, the grade and angle, who set it, and recent sends. It updates as burns happen.

BUILD A WORKOUT
Pick Volume, Pyramid, Ladder or Grade Focus, set a target grade and warm-up, preview the climbs, then send them to the wall one burn at a time.

TRACK YOUR CLIMBING
Record sends, flashes and attempts. Get a recap with duration, grade spread and your hardest send. Watch your sends and grades stack up week after week on the Progress tab.

EXPORT AND IMPORT
Send your session to Strava, save it to Apple Health, and import your past Kilter and Tension logbook from Aurora Climbing.

FIND MORE TO CLIMB
Follow your crew and setters, search climbers, and pin playlists from Discover.

FREE, NO ADS, NO SUBSCRIPTIONS
Open source on GitHub.
