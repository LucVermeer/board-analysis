Boardsesh 2.0 is a native Android app. Search a climb and light the holds on Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL over Bluetooth, and it holds the connection in the background so the holds stay lit when the screen sleeps. Start a session and everyone in it can send the next climb. See who is on the wall right now in the live feed. Build Volume, Pyramid, Ladder or Grade Focus workouts. Record sends and watch your progress stack up. Free, no ads, open source.
