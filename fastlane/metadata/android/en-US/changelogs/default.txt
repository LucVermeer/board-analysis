Boardsesh 2.0 is a full native rewrite. The old app wrapped the website; this one is native, so it connects faster, holds the Bluetooth link steadier, and keeps holds lit while you climb.

New in 2.0:
- Quicker, steadier board connection
- Native feel: smoother lists, faster search, background support
- Search, filter, and queue climbs, cycle with one tap
- Party Mode for your crew
- Logbook synced to Aurora Climbing for Kilter and Tension

Open source. No ads, no subscriptions.
