Boardsesh 2.0 is a native Android app. Search a climb and light the holds on Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL over Bluetooth. It holds the connection in the background so the holds stay lit when the screen sleeps. Walk up to any wall and a live feed shows what is lit right now. Start a session and everyone shares one queue. Build Volume, Pyramid, Ladder or Grade Focus workouts. Record sends and import your Aurora logbook. Free, no ads, open source.
