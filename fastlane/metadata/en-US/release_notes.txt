Boardsesh 2.0 is a full native rewrite. The old app wrapped the website in a shell; this one is built native, so it connects to your board faster, holds the Bluetooth link steadier, and keeps the holds lit while you climb.

New in 2.0:
- Quicker, more reliable board connection over Bluetooth
- A native feel throughout: smoother lists, faster search, real background support
- Search tens of thousands of community-set climbs and filter by grade, rating, and holds
- Build a queue and cycle through it with one tap, no phone fumbling between burns
- Run a shared session with Party Mode so your crew climbs the same list
- Log every attempt and send, synced to Aurora Climbing for Kilter and Tension

Open source. No ads, no subscriptions.
