Search a climb and watch your wall light the holds. Tap a problem, the holds glow, you pull on. Boardsesh connects your phone to your board over Bluetooth, so you spend your session climbing instead of reading numbers off a screen.

ONE APP FOR EVERY BOARD YOU CLIMB
Kilter, Tension and MoonBoard each have their own app for their own board. Boardsesh works across all of them, plus Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL, in one place. Search any climb and Boardsesh sends it to the wall over Bluetooth, lighting the holds. Full LED control on all seven boards.

The payoff of one app is the two things a single-board app can't give you: board history that's always on, and sessions you drive with your crew.

BOARD HISTORY, ALWAYS ON
Every board you connect to has a live feed of what's lit on that wall right now: the climb up at the moment, who lit it, the grade and angle, who set it, and recent sends. You don't start anything. It's just there when you're on that board, updating as burns happen.

SESSIONS WITH YOUR CREW
Start a session and you and your crew share one queue everyone can drive. Anyone sends the next climb to the wall: no driver to hand the phone around, no turn to wait for. Whoever's connected over Bluetooth lights it. When you're done you get a recap and tracking: sends and attempts, grade spread, your hardest send, and progress over time.

Run the session from your lock screen (iOS 17+)
Live Activities put the current climb, your place in the queue, and next/previous on the lock screen and Dynamic Island, so you flip climbs without opening the app.

Build a workout
Pick Volume, Pyramid, Ladder or Grade Focus, set a target grade and warm-up, preview the climbs, then send them to the wall one burn at a time.

Track your climbing
Record sends, flashes and attempts, get a recap with duration, grade spread and your hardest send, and see your sends and grades chart out on the Progress tab.

Bring your history with you
Save your session to Apple Health, and import your past Kilter and Tension logbook from Aurora Climbing.

Find more to climb
Follow your crew and setters, search climbers, and pin playlists from Discover.

Free, no ads, no subscriptions. Open source on GitHub.
