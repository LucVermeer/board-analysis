Search a climb and watch your wall light the holds. Tap a problem, the holds glow, you pull on. Boardsesh connects your phone to your board over Bluetooth, so you spend your session climbing instead of reading numbers off a screen.

WHAT YOU GET

Light the holds
Search any climb and Boardsesh sends it to the wall over Bluetooth, lighting the holds on Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard, Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL. Full LED control on all seven boards.

Why the app
On iPhone, Safari has no Web Bluetooth, so the native app is what connects to your board and lights the holds.

Climb with your crew
Start a session and everyone in it can send the next climb to the wall. No driver to hand the phone around, no turn to wait for. Whoever's connected over Bluetooth lights it.

See who's on the wall
A live feed shows the climb lit right now, who lit it, the grade and angle, who set it, and recent sends, updating as burns happen.

Run the session from your lock screen (iOS 17+)
Live Activities put the current climb, your place in the queue, and next/previous on the lock screen and Dynamic Island, so you flip climbs without opening the app.

Build a workout
Pick Volume, Pyramid, Ladder or Grade Focus, set a target grade and warm-up, preview the climbs, then send them to the wall one burn at a time.

Track your climbing
Record sends, flashes and attempts, get a recap with duration, grade spread and your hardest send, and see your sends and grades chart out on the Progress tab.

Export and import
Export your session to Strava and save it to Apple Health on iOS. Import your past Kilter and Tension logbook from Aurora Climbing.

Find more to climb
Follow your crew and setters, search climbers, and pin playlists from Discover.

Free, no ads, no subscriptions. Open source on GitHub.

Supported boards: Kilter, Tension, MoonBoard (2010, 2016, 2024, Masters 2017, Masters 2019), Decoy, Touchstone, Grasshopper and So iLL.

Boardsesh works with these boards and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Aurora Climbing or Moon Climbing.
